For your 2027 plan

Your ambition first, then your calendar.

  1. Find your big projects.

    Much of next year’s engagement is already written down: in your city’s strategic plan, in department plans and budgets, in the decisions scheduled for 2027. Read the plans, then talk to departments, before they come to you.

  2. Pick your goal.

    Reach more residents? Build trust? Give residents real influence? Reach the people you never hear? Or meet legal obligations? Most teams want a bit of each, but the goal you put first should shape what you ask, when you engage and report back.

  3. Set your participation target.

    Take your number from the calculator above and make it your target for the end of 2027.
