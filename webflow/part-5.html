Close the loop

How do you bring residents back?

  1. A hundred residents post an idea.

    Asking is half the work. Whether residents join your next project depends on what you do after they answer.

  2. When they hear nothing back, 14 take part in another project.

    The rest do not return. A project that ends in silence makes the next one harder.

  3. When their idea gets an official answer, 22 come back.

    Eight more in every hundred. Residents who get an answer are more likely to join the next project.

  4. Yet half of projects end without a clear follow-up.

    On Go Vocal, 47% of projects that asked residents for input had a follow-up. This is where your team can stand out.

  1. The decision takes months? Close the loop in two stages.

    Teams often ask us: the decision comes later, so what can we report now? Start with what you heard.

Go deeper: how sure are these numbers

Coming back means taking part in another project on the same platform at least 30 days later: 13.8% without an official answer, 22.3% with one. This is a correlation, and the gap changes by year: 15% against 26% in the first twelve months, 14% against 17% in the latest twelve.

A follow-up is a status change on an idea, an official answer, published results or an information phase after the participation phase. Counting only the first three, 17% of projects visibly closed the loop.
