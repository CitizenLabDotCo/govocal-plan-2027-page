Your 2027 plan

What a good year of participation looks like

Your target from step one, your topics from step two, on one calendar from January to December.

  1. Start with something always on.

    A proposals page or a panel, open all year, so residents always have a way in and never have to wait for the next project.

  2. Launch your big decisions in January and September.

    That is when residents show up. Keep the biggest consultations away from the weeks before the summer.

  3. Keep something open every quarter.

    A name, a contest, a public vote. Quick and simple, so no quarter stays empty and participation becomes a habit.

  4. Report back after every project.

    First what you heard, then what you decided. Every loop you close makes the next project easier.
